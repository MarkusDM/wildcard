# Cover Brief

## Goal

Create a 16:9 cover image that immediately communicates:

- fantasy 1v1 poker duel
- Arc / USDC credibility
- AI coaching as a premium feature

## Composition

- Center: glowing poker table seen from a slight top-down angle
- Left: player silhouette with warm gold magic accents
- Right: rival silhouette with cool blue arcane accents
- Foreground: 2 hole cards, USDC chip stack, and a subtle AI sigil or coach icon
- Title lockup: WILDCARD
- Subtitle: 1v1 Arc Poker with Paid AI Coaching

## Visual Tone

- Dark fantasy tavern mood
- Warm ember gold + cool arcane cyan contrast
- Premium, not cartoonish
- Clean enough to read in small thumbnail size

## Export

- PNG
- 16:9
- Aim for 1920x1080
