# WILDCARD Slide Outline

## Slide 1. Title

- WILDCARD
- 1v1 poker duels on Arc with paid AI coaching

## Slide 2. Problem

- Most Web3 game demos show wallets and contracts, but not a gameplay loop people would repeatedly use.
- AI tools are powerful, but most products still lack a clean way to monetize tiny, high-frequency agent actions.

## Slide 3. Solution

- WILDCARD is a fast 1v1 duel game with off-chain poker logic and Arc-based USDC settlement.
- We sell in-match AI coaching as gasless nanopaid actions instead of subscriptions or ads.

## Slide 4. Product Flow

- Deposit USDC
- Lock buy-in for a duel
- Play public, private, or versus agent
- Buy AI advice per call with x402-compatible flow

## Slide 5. Why Arc

- Arc is used for the money layer: deposit, lock, settle, withdraw.
- Gameplay stays off-chain, which keeps the match responsive and makes the system practical.

## Slide 6. Why Circle

- Circle Gateway and x402 let us price advice at micropayment scale.
- This makes “pay only when you ask the coach” viable.

## Slide 7. Live Features

- Public 1v1 matchmaking
- Private room codes
- Play vs agent
- Best move, risk, and magic advice
- Payment proof ledger and spend history

## Slide 8. Business Model

- Paid AI hints during matches
- Premium coaching packs later
- Tournament hosting and community ladders later

## Slide 9. Market / Users

- Competitive casual players
- Poker learners
- Strategy communities
- Streamers and creator-led game rooms

## Slide 10. Moat / Differentiation

- Not just another wallet demo
- Not just another poker clone
- The product combines gameplay retention, off-chain speed, Arc-native settlement, and monetized AI help

## Slide 11. Roadmap

- Real Circle credentials in production
- More advanced agent models
- Rankings, tournaments, and friend invites
- Mobile-first onboarding

## Slide 12. Closing

- WILDCARD turns AI advice into a native game mechanic with real payment rails underneath.
